From clay to your shelf

Five stages, most of them still done exactly as they were fifty years ago.

01

Dig & knead

Raw clay is sourced locally and kneaded by foot to remove air pockets.

02

Throw on the wheel

Shaped by hand on a manually spun potter's wheel, one piece at a time.

03

Sun-dry

Left to dry slowly in open air over two to four days, weather permitting.

04

Fire in the kiln

Wood-fired at high heat to harden the form and set the base tone.

05

Paint & finish

Glazed, dyed or left bare, then hand-finished and checked piece by piece.

Started at a family kiln in 2011, still run the same way

Kaarigar began as a two-room workshop where one family threw pots for the neighbourhood. Today we work with 42 artisan households across Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan and Gujarat — but the pieces are still shaped, dried and fired the same slow way they always were.

No two pieces leave the kiln identical. We think that's the point: a small crack in the glaze, a slightly uneven rim — these are signatures, not flaws.

— Team Kaarigar
